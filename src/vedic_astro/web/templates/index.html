Sibling Count

R.G. Rao Chart 1 (PAGE 6): Jupiter represents the native. A planet in the sign immediately before Jupiter is an elder sibling; every other planet crossed going forward from Jupiter's sign (before reaching that same previous sign again) is a younger sibling, in crossing order.

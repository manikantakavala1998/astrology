Rules & calculations

Count signs from the planet's natal sign as 1. Every classical planet aspects the 7th sign. Mars also aspects the 4th and 8th; Jupiter the 5th and 9th; Saturn the 3rd and 10th.

Aspected signs includes empty signs. This table follows zodiac signs independently of house-system selection. Rahu and Ketu can receive aspects; their outgoing Vedic aspects are not enabled.

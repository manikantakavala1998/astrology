# Laghu Parashari workspace

The Parasara Method navigation includes a dedicated Laghu Parashari workspace.
All twelve ascendants use classical seven-planet sign ownership and Whole Sign
D1 houses, independent of the selected cusp-based house system.

The 42-topic catalogue follows the four-part text: foundations (1–13), Raja Yoga
(14–22), Maraka (23–28), dasha/antardasha (29–42). Opening material is reading
context. Conditional rules identify their prerequisite instead of manufacturing
an event or score. Every calculated result includes its house/planet arithmetic.

## Explicit implementation conventions

Benefic ownership, adverse ownership, Kendra conditions and Maraka roles are
separate flags. Mixed trine/adverse ownership stays mixed. The 8th-lord exception
applies to Lagna ownership and to Sun/Moon. The six conventional standalone
Yogakarakas require ownership of H5/H9 and H4/H7/H10. Lagna plus another angle
receives Lagna support, with the distinction visible in the conventions.
Ascendant classifications are derived from these rules, rather than importing a
BPHS/Jataka Chandrika appendix or a K. N. Rao ascendant table into this method.

Sambandha means same-sign conjunction, mutual classical graha aspect, or mutual
sign exchange. A one-way aspect is shown in the aspect table but is not promoted
to mutual sambandha. No Rahu/Ketu aspect or co-lordship is assigned. Their house,
dispositor and companions are exposed; yoga association remains conditional.

The existing Vimshottari engine supplies all major/subperiod boundaries. Period
readings describe supportive/challenging/mixed tendencies, with matched rules,
ownership, relationship and natal dignity. They are not event guarantees.
Saturn–Venus mutual-period rules remain an explicit exception; no simplistic
good/bad reversal is invented.

The text does not supply a numerical strong-planet cutoff for verse 17. Existing
Shadbala measurements remain separate. Verses 25–27 and 39 require a lifespan
prerequisite which this workspace does not establish. Technical Maraka candidates
and Saturn connections are computed; no death date or lifespan is inferred.
Raja Yoga onset/continuation rules remain conditional on the natal yoga and
its manifestation. The catalogue states these limits in the calculation result.
